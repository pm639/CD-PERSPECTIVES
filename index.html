About

Carolina Doria

Creative Director | Art Advisor
MA in Art and Design History, Parsons School of Design graduate

Carolina Doria is a New York–based art advisor and creative director working across art, design, and visual culture. She works with private collectors, brands, and creative projects, bringing a discerning eye, careful research, and a deep understanding of each client’s vision.

Of Brazilian and Italian heritage, Carolina draws on her travels as the foundation of her research, shaping a sensitivity to context, craftsmanship, and the dialogue between past and present. Her work is defined by thoughtful selection, strong visual narratives, and experiences built to last.
